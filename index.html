Gabriella Gonzalez's Dodgers Obsession Page

  • I absolutely love the L.A. Dodgers! I have been watching them play since I was little, and I have gone to over a dozen Fourth of July games.
  • My favorite player is Freddie Freeman. He is a first baseman and one of the best hitters in baseball.
  • The Dodgers have won a total of 8 World Series championships.
  • The team was named the Dodgers in 1895 because Brooklyn residents had to dodge fast-moving trolley cars.
  • Before settling on Dodgers, the team went through several nicknames, including the Grays, Bridegrooms, Superbas, Robins, and even "Dem Bums."
  • Babe Ruth is one of the most famous baseball players in history, although he did not play for the Dodgers.
